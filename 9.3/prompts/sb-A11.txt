A single storyboard sheet in a tall nine-by-sixteen vertical proportion.

This is ONE single page, not a sheet of panels: draw NO panel borders, NO frames, NO gutters, NO divisions of any kind, and no second panel anywhere. The drawing fills the whole sheet edge to edge.

A storyboard sheet for a poster and magazine-editorial music video, drawn as flat anime-style illustration: clean ink line work, flat cel shading with no soft gradient anywhere, a coarse open halftone dot screen carrying every mid value, visible paper grain and a letterpress bite. Three inks only - an oatmeal off-white paper ground, a dense charcoal-black ink, and one vermilion accent. Not photorealistic, not live action, not a 3D render, not clean vector art, not airbrushed. Light is a hard-edged plane split by a single crisp boundary, never a gradient. No flag, no national emblem, no rising-sun rays, no crest and no military insignia anywhere on the sheet, and at most one plain disc or one brush ring in any single panel.

NO PEOPLE APPEAR ON THIS SHEET AT ALL. There is no figure, no face, no body and no part of a body in any panel. Do not invent characters to fill the space; empty paper and printed type are the intended content.

Every panel carries one small solid black square rule centred at its top edge and a run of short horizontal ink dashes and dots ranged down one margin as texture. Those dashes and dots are marks, not words: they must form no letter of any alphabet and no numeral.

Only the exact strings named panel by panel below may appear as readable text on this sheet. Every other readable word, label, sign, watermark, subtitle, brand, numeral, focal-length value, timestamp and invented character is forbidden.

NO FILLED CIRCLE ON A PALE GROUND ANYWHERE ON THIS SHEET. Do not draw a solid red or vermilion disc on oatmeal paper, on white, or on any pale surface, at any size, in any panel - not as a graphic element, and not printed on any object: no label, bottle, can, packet, sign, banner, box, badge, flag, fan, lantern or garment carries a red circle. A solid vermilion disc is permitted ONLY where it sits on a solid charcoal-black ground. An open brush ring drawn in one stroke is unaffected and stays permitted. Any object that would normally carry a printed emblem is drawn with that emblem left off entirely, and any object that would normally carry writing has its writing left blank or reduced to abstract marks that spell nothing in any script.

Each panel below sets its text a DIFFERENT way and stages its figure a DIFFERENT way. Do not carry one panel's typographic layout into the next, and do not fall back on one default arrangement for every string: follow each panel's own stated layout exactly, whether that is a horizontal row, a curve, a stair, a split, a stack or a column. Panels that name an object or a place must show it; panels that name none are deliberately empty and must stay empty.
