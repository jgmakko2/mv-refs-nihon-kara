A storyboard sheet for a poster and magazine-editorial music video, drawn as flat anime-style illustration: clean ink line work, flat cel shading with no soft gradient anywhere, a coarse open halftone dot screen carrying every mid value, visible paper grain and a letterpress bite. Three inks only - an oatmeal off-white paper ground, a dense charcoal-black ink, and one vermilion accent. Not photorealistic, not live action, not a 3D render, not clean vector art, not airbrushed.
