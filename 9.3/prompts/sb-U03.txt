A single storyboard sheet in a tall nine-by-sixteen vertical proportion.

Lay the sheet out as a strict two-by-two grid of four equal panels, read left to right along the top row and then left to right along the bottom row. Each panel is the same size and the same tall proportion as the sheet itself. Separate them with a thin plain gutter of bare paper and nothing else.

A storyboard sheet for a poster and magazine-editorial music video, drawn as flat anime-style illustration: clean ink line work, flat cel shading with no soft gradient anywhere, a coarse open halftone dot screen carrying every mid value, visible paper grain and a letterpress bite. Three inks only - an oatmeal off-white paper ground, a dense charcoal-black ink, and one vermilion accent. Not photorealistic, not live action, not a 3D render, not clean vector art, not airbrushed. Light is a hard-edged plane split by a single crisp boundary, never a gradient. No flag, no national emblem, no rising-sun rays, no crest and no military insignia anywhere on the sheet, and at most one plain disc or one brush ring in any single panel.

Where a figure appears she is always the same young woman: a chin-length blunt bob of solid black hair that splits into strands at the fringe with two longer locks in front of the ears, a neutral face with level black eyes, a small straight nose and a closed mouth, one oversized white shirt buttoned to the throat with a sharp collar and dropped shoulders, and floor-length wide black trousers. Her value structure is fixed: hair solid black, shirt an open perforated halftone that lets the paper show through, trousers solid black. Do not redesign her and do not add accessories.

Every panel carries one small solid black square rule centred at its top edge and a run of short horizontal ink dashes and dots ranged down one margin as texture. Those dashes and dots are marks, not words: they must form no letter of any alphabet and no numeral.

Only the exact strings named panel by panel below may appear as readable text on this sheet. Every other readable word, label, sign, watermark, subtitle, brand, numeral, focal-length value, timestamp and invented character is forbidden.

NO FILLED CIRCLE ON A PALE GROUND ANYWHERE ON THIS SHEET. Do not draw a solid red or vermilion disc on oatmeal paper, on white, or on any pale surface, at any size, in any panel - not as a graphic element, and not printed on any object: no label, bottle, can, packet, sign, banner, box, badge, flag, fan, lantern or garment carries a red circle. A solid vermilion disc is permitted ONLY where it sits on a solid charcoal-black ground. An open brush ring drawn in one stroke is unaffected and stays permitted. Any object that would normally carry a printed emblem is drawn with that emblem left off entirely, and any object that would normally carry writing has its writing left blank or reduced to abstract marks that spell nothing in any script.

Each panel below sets its text a DIFFERENT way and stages its figure a DIFFERENT way. Do not carry one panel's typographic layout into the next, and do not fall back on one default arrangement for every string: follow each panel's own stated layout exactly, whether that is a horizontal row, a curve, a stair, a split, a stack or a column. Panels that name an object or a place must show it; panels that name none are deliberately empty and must stay empty.

Panel 1: the ground is a solid charcoal-black ink page. three flat black strobe silhouettes of the same figure repeated across a solid ink page at receding sizes, carrying no words of any kind. In this panel there is a crosswalk of parallel white bars under all three silhouettes. The figure is present in this panel exactly as described above. Her posture in this panel: three silhouettes in three different postures - one crouched, one at the top of a leap with both feet off the ground, one landing - striding sideways in sequence and stopping hard. Her design does not change - the same bob, the same halftone shirt, the same three-value structure. Only her posture and the camera angle are different here. This panel carries no readable text of any kind.

Panel 2: the ground is bare oatmeal off-white paper. a type-only page with no figure and no part of a body anywhere in it. In this panel there is an office ceiling grid of fluorescent tubes behind the type. Draw it as a real place with recession and depth, not as a flat pattern - it is the only thing in this panel that has space in it. There is no figure, no face and no part of a body in this panel. How the text is SET in this panel: The same two words are PRINTED NINE TIMES down the panel at the same size and in the same position on each line, and the ink gets lighter with every repeat until the last one is only a ghost of itself. The topmost repeat is solid and completely readable. This panel carries the words JAPAN MISSION, set in condensed black capitals, spelled exactly that way and nothing else.

Panel 3: the ground is a solid charcoal-black ink page. three flat black silhouettes of the same figure printed three times across the page. In this panel there is a vending machine's lit grid of identical cans behind the silhouettes. The figure is present in this panel exactly as described above. Her posture in this panel: three silhouettes in three different postures - crouched, mid-leap and landing - spinning on the spot one after another. Her design does not change - the same bob, the same halftone shirt, the same three-value structure. Only her posture and the camera angle are different here. How the text is SET in this panel: The two words are OVERPRINTED TWICE as a misregistered second pass: once in black and once in vermilion, the vermilion copy offset a few millimetres down and to the right of the black one, so the pair reads as a printing press that has slipped. Both copies are complete and the words stay readable. This panel carries the words JAPAN DREAM, set in condensed black capitals, spelled exactly that way and nothing else.

Panel 4: the ground is one flat vermilion field owning the whole panel. the head and shoulders in right profile as a flat black silhouette. In this panel there is overhead power lines crossing the sky behind the profile. The figure is present in this panel exactly as described above. Her posture in this panel: the profile silhouette with its head thrown back, hair swinging out in one flat arc. Her design does not change - the same bob, the same halftone shirt, the same three-value structure. Only her posture and the camera angle are different here. How the text is SET in this panel: The two words are printed TWICE IN THE SAME PANEL in opposite polarities, one above the other: the upper pair as black letters directly on the colour field, the lower pair as letters knocked out of a solid black bar so they read as the paper colour - the same words in both states at once, so the panel shows the blink rather than describing it. A third, much smaller pair sits beneath them at a third of the size. This panel carries the words JAPAN MIRACLE, set in condensed black capitals, spelled exactly that way and nothing else.

Draw NO arrows, NO motion lines, NO speed lines, NO panel numbers, NO labels, NO captions and NO annotations of any kind anywhere on the sheet. Nothing on this sheet describes movement; it is a set of still compositions.

Only this one woman appears anywhere on the sheet. Do not add a second character, a crowd, a line-up or any other person.
